# Plans and pricing

FluenzR has a single plan, **Pro**, and there is no free plan any more.

## The Pro plan

**29 EUR excl. VAT per month** (34.80 EUR incl. VAT), or **290 EUR excl. VAT per year**.

It includes:

- unlimited email, within what your provider allows (Gmail, Outlook, SMTP): mailboxes, contacts and sends with no cap set by FluenzR;
- mailbox warming, A/B tests, CRM and Inbox;
- **1 LinkedIn account included**: profile visit, invitation, message, InMail and engagement (see [LinkedIn](/docs/linkedin));
- connecting an AI (Claude, ChatGPT) through the MCP server (see [MCP](/docs/api/mcp)).

## Extra LinkedIn accounts

Each LinkedIn account beyond the first costs **10 EUR excl. VAT per month** (12 EUR incl. VAT). Example: 3 LinkedIn accounts = 29 + 2 x 10 = 49 EUR excl. VAT per month.

## 14-day trial

- You try the Pro plan for 14 days.
- No card is asked for email. A card is requested **only to connect a LinkedIn account** during the trial; it is not charged before the trial ends.
- If you do not subscribe when the trial ends, the account is paused: your data is kept, but nothing is sent.

## LANCEMENT19 code

The code **LANCEMENT19** takes 10 EUR excl. VAT off per month, for life, on the monthly subscription (19 EUR excl. VAT per month). It is limited to the first 20 users and does not apply to the annual plan.

## Subscribing and managing your subscription

1. Open your **Profile** (bottom-left icon).
2. Click **Manage my subscription**.
3. Pay or change your subscription through Stripe: invoices, payment method and cancellation are available there.

You can cancel at any time, with no commitment.

## Earlier subscribers

Subscribers on an older price keep it, with 1 LinkedIn account included. Existing free accounts keep their access.
