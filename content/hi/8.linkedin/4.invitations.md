# Received invitations

When someone invites you on LinkedIn, the **Welcome my LinkedIn invitations** automation accepts the invitation, then sends your message shortly after.

## Create the automation

1. Open **LinkedIn**, **Automations** tab, then **Received invitations**.
2. Choose the LinkedIn account. There is one received-invitations automation per account.
3. Choose **Who to accept**: everyone, only if the job title contains one of your keywords, or everyone except those keywords.
4. Set the number of acceptances per day (1 to 100) and the delay before the first message.
5. Write your messages (a follow-up after a few days is possible and stops as soon as the person replies), or use an existing campaign whose first step is a LinkedIn message.

Nothing is sent until the campaign runs: a campaign created here is a draft.

## Good to know

- A set-aside invitation is never declined: it stays pending on LinkedIn and you can answer it yourself.
- Unsubscribed or blacklisted people are never accepted.
- Each automation card shows the counters (received, accepted, set aside, messages sent) and the latest invitations with the reason for any exclusion.
