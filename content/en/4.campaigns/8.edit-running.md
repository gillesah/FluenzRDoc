# Edit a running campaign

You can add steps to a campaign that is **running or paused**, without recreating it or losing its contacts. A campaign that has not been launched yet can be edited freely, as before.

## Adding steps

1. Open the campaign, **Workflow** tab.
2. Add your steps between two existing steps. Accepted: email, wait, LinkedIn step, call and manual task. You can also place them on the "yes" or "no" exit of a condition.
3. A message says "N step(s) added, not saved yet". Click **Review and save** (or **Discard additions**).
4. Read the preview, then click **Add the steps**.

An added step must be complete: an email has a subject and a text, a LinkedIn message has a text, a wait lasts at least one unit and at most 90 days.

## What the preview shows

Before saving, FluenzR computes the effect on the contacts currently in progress:

- how many will go through the new steps (those upstream of the insertion point);
- how many will not: they are already further along, or waiting right after;
- if the steps are on a branch, that only contacts taking that branch will go through them;
- if no contact is in progress, that the new steps will apply to the next contacts.

## Contacts never go backwards

A contact that has passed the insertion point does not go back: it does not go through the new steps and nothing is sent again.

For contacts **waiting right after** the new steps, you decide. By default they do not go through them. If you tick **Send the contacts waiting right after through the new steps**, those that have not done anything on that step yet are put on the first new step. A wait already in progress is never shortened. If the step right after has several entries, FluenzR cannot tell where those contacts came from and does not move them.

## Editing the content of existing steps

Texts and settings of existing steps can still be edited. Two effects to know:

- if you change the weights or the number of variants of an A/B test, the comparison in progress is skewed;
- if you change a condition, the preview says how many contacts will be evaluated right away.

## What is not possible yet

- **Deleting** a step from a running campaign: contacts are positioned on it.
- **Adding a condition**, or changing the type of an existing step.
- **Rewiring** the links between steps: only adding a step between two steps is possible.

For these changes, create a new campaign.

## History and conflicts

Each change to a running campaign is saved as a workflow version, with the number of contacts moved. If the campaign was modified in the meantime (for example from another tab), FluenzR refuses the save: "The campaign was modified in the meantime. Redo the addition on the up-to-date version."
