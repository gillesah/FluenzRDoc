# Calls and manual tasks

A sequence can pass through you: a call to make, a letter to send. FluenzR calls nobody and sends nothing in your place: it puts a "to do" task in front of you and waits for you to handle it before going on.

## Adding a step

In the workflow editor, click **Add a call** or **Add a task**.

**Call step**
- **Call brief or script** (optional): a text to guide you, with the same variables as emails (`{firstName}`, `{company}`...).
- **If no result is entered after**: a delay in days. After that delay the call is marked "not done" and the sequence continues on the default branch.

**Manual task**
- **What to do**: for example send a paper brochure or a gift, or prepare a quote.
- Same delay: with no action after N days, the task is marked "not done" and the sequence continues.

## Where to find them

The task appears in the **Today's calls and tasks** card on the Home and on the contact page (**To do for this contact**). For a call, the contact's number is clickable; with no number, add it on their page. You can also see the contact's latest exchanges and notes before calling.

## Entering the result of a call

After the call, click the result:

| Result | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Reached, interested | The contact answered and wants to know more |
| Reached, not interested | The contact answered and declines |
| Voicemail | You reached an answering machine |
| No answer | Nobody picked up |
| Wrong number | The number is not the right one |
| Meeting booked | A meeting is agreed |

The result is added to the contact's call log and the sequence resumes. **Meeting booked** acts like a booking on your booking page: the contact's sequences stop and they move to the "Meeting booked" pipeline stage.

For a manual task, click **Done** or **Skip**: the sequence continues either way.

## Choosing what follows from the result

After the call, add a **Call result** condition and tick at least one result: those follow the "yes" branch, the others (and a call not made in time) the "no" branch. The answer is known as soon as you enter the result, with no waiting. A call used by a condition cannot be deleted before that condition.

## Dictated or typed debrief

On a call to make, you can write a debrief instead of choosing the result directly.

1. Dictate it (2 minutes at most) with the **Dictate** button, or type it (4,000 characters at most).
2. Click **Analyse**. The AI suggests a summary, the objections, the next step, a follow-up date and the call result.
3. Review, correct if needed, then click **Confirm and save**. The confirmed debrief is saved as a note on the contact's page.

> **Privacy**
> The call itself is never recorded. Only your dictated note is transcribed, in memory: it is neither kept nor logged. Only the debrief you confirm is saved.

The number of debriefs analysed is limited to 100 per month per account; the counter is shown. Beyond that, or if dictation is unavailable (microphone denied, unsupported browser), type your note or enter the result directly.

## With an AI connected through MCP

The `list_call_tasks` and `complete_call_task` tools let a connected AI list the calls and tasks to do and complete them (see [MCP](/docs/api/mcp)).
