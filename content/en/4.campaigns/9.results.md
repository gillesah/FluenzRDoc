# Results tab

In **Campaigns**, the **Results** tab (next to "All" and "Fed continuously") gathers the figures of all your campaigns. Pick 7, 30 or 90 days: every figure is compared with the previous period of the same length.

## What you will find

- **Key figures**: emails sent, prospects reached, replies, bounce rate, unsubscribes, LinkedIn invitations accepted, with the trend.
- **Activity per day**: sends (email and LinkedIn), then replies.
- **Email sequence, step by step**: how many prospects receive email 1, 2, 3, and what each step triggers.
- **LinkedIn, from visit to reply**.
- **Campaigns compared**: reply, bounce and unsubscribe rates, with the detail "3/37". A "low volume" label appears under 50 prospects: a single message then moves the rate by several points.
- **Email and LinkedIn** side by side, **what the replies say** (Inbox status, message nature) and the state of your **sending mailboxes**.

Every chart has a "View as table" button.

## How it is counted

- **A reply** is a prospect who answered and whose sequence stopped, whatever the channel. A reply received after the sequence ended is not counted here: it stays visible in the Inbox.
- **An unsubscribe** only counts if the prospect received at least one message from the campaign before. A prospect who had already unsubscribed when enrolled, taken out of the sequence without receiving anything, is not one.
- Days are Paris days.

## Who sees what

You see your own campaigns and those your organization has shared, as in the campaign list. A solo account sees its own.

## Deliberately left out

**Opens** and **clicks** are not shown: the tracking pixel is off by default and those figures would be wrong. They were also removed from a campaign's Metrics tab. Meetings coming from a campaign will appear once their link to the campaign is reliable.

## Unsubscribe date and origin

FluenzR now records the date of every unsubscribe and where it came from: the prospect (link), you (in the app), the API, or a "stop" reply.
