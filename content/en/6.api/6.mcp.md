# Connect an AI (MCP)

The FluenzR connector uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Once connected, an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT can read your FluenzR account and prepare your work: contacts, groups, campaigns, replies, meetings. The AI prepares, you decide what goes out.

The connector is included in the Pro plan, trial included. It is the same account and the same data as the app: nothing is copied elsewhere.

## Server address

```
https://app.fluenzr.co/api/mcp
```

You sign in with your FluenzR account (OAuth 2.1): no API key to copy. The page **Settings > AI > Connection** in the app shows this address and the steps below.

## Connect your AI

### Claude.ai and Claude Desktop

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Click **Add custom connector**.
3. Paste the server address above.
4. Sign in to FluenzR when the authorization window opens, then click **Authorize**.

### Claude Code

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http fluenzr https://app.fluenzr.co/api/mcp
```

In Claude Code, type `/mcp`, pick `fluenzr` and authenticate.

### ChatGPT

1. In ChatGPT, open `chatgpt.com/plugins`, click **Add** (top right), then **Add a custom MCP server**.
2. Give it a name (for example FluenzR), paste the server address, keep authentication on **OAuth**, tick the confirmation box, then click **Create as plugin**.
3. Click **Connect**: the FluenzR page opens. Sign in (password or Google), then click **Authorize**.
4. In a new conversation, type `@FluenzR` and pick the plugin. Mention `@FluenzR` in every new conversation.

ChatGPT's interface changes often: if the labels differ, look for the option to add a custom MCP server. In ChatGPT's settings you can also ask it to confirm before each action.

### Another MCP client

Any client that supports remote MCP servers over HTTP (Streamable HTTP) with OAuth can connect:

1. Add a remote MCP server and give it the address `https://app.fluenzr.co/api/mcp`.
2. The client registers itself (dynamic client registration) and opens the FluenzR sign-in page.
3. Sign in, read the permissions listed, then click **Authorize**. **Deny** cancels the connection.

### What you authorize

The authorization window lists the permissions (scopes) requested by the AI:

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| `mcp:read` | Read your contacts, campaigns, mailboxes and statistics |
| `mcp:contacts` | Create contacts and contact groups |
| `mcp:campaigns` | Create, start and pause email campaigns |
| `mcp:full` | Everything above (never ticked by default) |

You can untick a permission before authorizing. Without `mcp:campaigns`, for example, the AI can read and import contacts but cannot create or start a campaign.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `search_linkedin_people` | Search LinkedIn people through your connected account. Nothing is saved |
| `add_linkedin_results_to_group` | Add chosen profiles to a contact group |

Safeguards stay active whatever the AI asks: a campaign is refused when it would send nothing (no audience, no usable mailbox), when a sending domain has no SPF or DMARC, or when an email has more than 2 links or a URL shortener. The first 20 recipients go out alone and the campaign pauses if more than 10 % bounce.

## Example requests

> Check my account and tell me what is missing before I can send.

The AI calls `get_account_status` and lists the steps, with a direct link for those that need you.

> List my mailboxes and tell me how many emails I can send per day.

> Show me the replies marked "to handle" and draft an answer to the first one. Do not send anything.

> Import these 40 contacts into a new group "Bakeries Lyon", then prepare a three-email campaign for this group. Show me the sequence before you create it.

> Which of my campaigns has the best open rate this month? Give me the numbers per step.

> Pause the campaign "Autumn offer" and tell me how many contacts are still waiting.

> Search LinkedIn for founders of marketing agencies in France and add the first results to a group.

## Starter prompt

Once the AI is connected, paste this message into the conversation:

> You are connected to my FluenzR account. I want to do as little as possible: you handle everything.
> Start with get_account_status: do the AI steps yourself, and for the ones that need me, give me the direct link.
> Then ask me the questions from get_onboarding_questions, one or two at a time, to set up my account and my first campaign as well as possible.
> Before each new campaign, call get_campaign_brief and ask me the questions that are still open, two or three at a time, then restate the brief for my approval before create_campaign.
> Follow the advice in the responses: if a mailbox is new or warming up, start with very few sends.
> Never ask me for a password in the conversation. Show me the sequence before calling start_campaign, and every reply to a contact before send_reply.

## The "Validate my AI's actions before sending" setting

This box is on the page **Settings > AI > Connection** and on the page **Settings > AI > To approve**.

| Box | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ticked** | Every sending action of your connected AI waits for your approval on the **To approve** page: campaign launch, restart of a paused campaign that was never approved, text change on a running campaign, reply to a prospect. Nothing is sent before you approve. |
| **Unticked** | The same actions go out right away. |

What happens when it is ticked:

- **Launch**: `start_campaign` puts the campaign in "awaiting approval". You see the rendered emails and a sample of contacts on **To approve**, then approve or refuse. If you refuse, the campaign goes back to draft with your reason, which the AI can read.
- **Text change on a running campaign**: sending is suspended until you compare the old and the new text and approve. Refusing restores the old text.
- **Insert a step in a running campaign**: same approval. The AI can first ask for an impact preview (`dryRun`), which saves nothing.
- **Reply to a contact**: `send_reply` saves a draft on **To approve**. You read it, edit it and send it, or refuse it. The AI cannot approve for itself.

Good to know:

- The box is **unticked by default for a new account**. Accounts created before that change keep their setting. Check it before your first campaign.
- When you untick it, FluenzR asks you to confirm and warns that what your AI sends goes out without your agreement. Server safeguards (targeting, limits, DNS) stay active.
- Resuming a campaign you already approved and that was only paused restarts directly, without a new approval.
- The AI cannot change this setting. Only you can, from the app.
