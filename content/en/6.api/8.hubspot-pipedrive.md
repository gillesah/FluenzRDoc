# HubSpot and Pipedrive

FluenzR connects directly to HubSpot and Pipedrive, with no Zapier in between. Set it up in **Settings > Integrations**, on the **HubSpot** or **Pipedrive** tab.

## Connect your CRM

The connection uses a token you create in your CRM. The token is checked, then stored encrypted in FluenzR; it is never shown again or sent by email.

### HubSpot (private app token)

1. In HubSpot: **Settings** (the gear) > **Integrations** > **Private Apps** > **Create private app**.
2. Name it "FluenzR". In the **Scopes** tab, tick: `crm.objects.contacts.read`, `crm.objects.contacts.write`, `crm.lists.read`, `crm.objects.deals.read`, `crm.objects.deals.write`, `crm.schemas.deals.read`.
3. Click **Create app**, copy the **access token** and paste it into FluenzR.

### Pipedrive (personal API token)

1. In Pipedrive: click your name at the top right > **Personal preferences** > **API** tab.
2. Copy your personal API token.
3. Paste it into FluenzR. It carries the rights of your Pipedrive account.

## Import contacts

In **Import contacts from the CRM**, load your lists (HubSpot: static or dynamic lists) or people filters (Pipedrive), pick one and run the import.

- Contacts join a new FluenzR group named after the list.
- The email address is the key: a contact already in FluenzR is never duplicated.
- Contacts without an email are skipped. Up to 3,000 contacts per import.

## What FluenzR sends to the CRM

Three options, saved as soon as you tick them:

| Option | Effect in the CRM |
|--------|-------------------|
| Contacts who reply, book a meeting or are marked interested | The contact is created if it does not exist. |
| Activity: email sent, reply received, meeting booked | A note is added to the contact record (contact created if needed). |
| Create a deal when a prospect is interested | One deal per contact, linked to its record, in the pipeline and stage you choose. |

The email address is the deduplication key: a contact already in the CRM is found, never duplicated, and FluenzR never modifies its fields.

## Follow the sync

The tab shows **pending**, **sent** and **failed** counts over 7 days, the date of the last successful sync and the list of failed sends with the reason. No alert email is sent: everything is read here.

- A temporary CRM problem (outage, rate limit) is retried automatically, up to 6 times.
- If the token is refused (expired, revoked), syncing stops and the tab shows **Reconnect needed**. Create a new token and reconnect: failed sends restart by themselves.
- **Test connection** checks the token at any time; **Disconnect** deletes the token from FluenzR (nothing is deleted in the CRM).
