# Email footers

The email footer is the block that ends a message, under the mailbox signature. By default it is the unsubscribe link. You can choose another footer for the whole account, then change it campaign by campaign.

## Create your footers

1. Open **Settings** → **My profile**, then the **Email footers** tab.
2. Create a footer: give it a **name** (to find it again) and type its **text**.
3. To offer an unsubscribe link in your text, insert the `{{unsubscribeLink}}` variable. It is optional.
4. Choose the **account default footer**, then save.

The default footer applies to every campaign that does not pick another one, and to direct emails sent outside a campaign.

## Choose a footer for a campaign

In the campaign editor, open **Settings**, then the **Email footer** field. Five choices:

| Choice | What is added to the message |
|---|---|
| **The account's footer** | The default footer set in **My profile** |
| **Unsubscribe link** | The sentence with the personal unsubscribe link |
| **"Reply stop" sentence** | A sentence inviting the recipient to reply "stop" |
| **Your own footer** | One of your custom footers |
| **No footer** | Nothing |

## No footer

With **No footer**, nothing is added to the message, and opens of the email are not tracked.

This choice is the sender's, who remains responsible for complying with the prospecting rules.

## What does not change

- **"Stop" replies** are still detected: the contact leaves the sequence, whatever the footer.
- **A footer deleted while in use** is replaced by the unsubscribe link: no campaign ends up without a footer by accident.
- **In "Send without trace"**, the `{{unsubscribeLink}}` variable of a custom footer is replaced by the "reply stop" sentence. See [Footer, tracking and Send without trace](/en/docs/help/deliverability).
- **The test email** has no footer.
