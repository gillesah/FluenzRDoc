# Alerts

**Settings > Alerts** lets you choose what FluenzR tells you about, and where.

Each row has two boxes: **In the app** (the bell at the top of the screen) and **By email**.

## What you get by default

If you change nothing, only the essential emails are sent:

- **A prospect replies** (email or LinkedIn): bell and email.
- **Meeting booked, rescheduled or canceled**: email copy (as before).
- **Blocked sending** (mailbox deactivated, campaign without a sending mailbox, campaign paused or failing) and **LinkedIn blocked** (account to reconnect or restricted, actions failing): bell and email, **always on**. These alerts mean nothing is going out: you cannot turn them off, only change the address that receives them.
- The others (prospect marked interested, reply syncing down, LinkedIn automations stopped, hooks to review): bell only. Tick "By email" if you also want them by email, when the option exists.

## The address that receives alerts

By default, alert emails go to your account address. To receive them elsewhere (a team address, for example):

1. Enter the address under **Another address** and click **Send the confirmation link**.
2. Open the email received at that address (sent by FluenzR, link valid for 24 hours) and click the link.
3. The address is confirmed: alerts arrive there.

Until the link is opened, alerts keep going to the previous address. **Go back to the account address** cancels the chosen address.

Password, billing and trial emails are always sent to your account address.
