# Account delegation

Delegation lets an outside person (an assistant, an agency, a freelancer) act in your FluenzR account without knowing your password. You invite them by email. They have their own FluenzR account and switch into yours when they need to. You can remove the access whenever you want, and it stops right away.

Only one person can have access to your account at a time.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Access to my account**.
2. In **Email address of the person**, type their address.
3. Click **Give access**.

The person receives an email with a link. The screen then shows **Invitation sent, valid for 7 days**. Once they accept, it shows **Access active since** followed by the date, then the date of their **Last sign-in**.

If they did not get the email, click **Resend the invitation**. The new link replaces the old one: the old link stops working, and the 7 days start over.

You can send 5 invitations per 24-hour period (resends count too).

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the link from the email. The screen shows who is giving you access to their account.
2. Click **Sign in to continue**, or **Create my account to continue** if you do not have a FluenzR account yet.
3. Click **Accept**. You can also click **Decline**.

Good to know:

- **The invitation is valid for 7 days.** After that, ask the person to send you a new one.
- **You must be signed in with the invited address.** With another address, FluenzR says the invitation is not for your email address.
- **Only the latest link works**: if the person sent the invitation again, use the latest email.

## Act in the account, then go back

Once you have accepted, open your profile menu. The **Accounts I manage** section appears there, with an **Act for** entry followed by the owner's name. Click it.

A **You are acting for** banner, followed by the owner's address, stays on screen while you are in their account, so you always know where you are. To go back to your own account, click **Back to my account** in the banner.

## What the invited person can do

They act on your behalf, inside your account: they can send emails, view and edit your contacts, create and launch campaigns, reply in the inbox, use LinkedIn, and manage your templates, lists and connected mailboxes.

Alerts (prospect replies, outages) keep going to your address, not theirs.

## What they cannot do

These actions stay with the account owner:

- change the subscription or billing;
- change the account password or email address;
- delete the account;
- create or manage API keys, webhooks or API integrations;
- manage the team and the organization;
- give or remove access (the person cannot invite someone in turn);
- delete a connected mailbox;
- erase the contacts of a list along with the list (deleting the list alone is still possible);
- connect, reconnect or delete a LinkedIn account;
- change enrichment keys, CRM connections and the Calendly connection;
- create or activate a tracking domain;
- change the prospecting rules or ask for a trial extension;
- open a support ticket in your name.

If they try, FluenzR shows: "This action isn't available while you act for another account."

## Who can be invited

Delegation is meant for an **outside person**. FluenzR therefore refuses an invitation in these cases:

- **Same company**: the address has the same email domain as yours (for example two addresses at `@mycompany.com`). A Gmail or Outlook address does not count as a company domain. The invitation is still possible if the person has their own paid subscription.
- **Same organization**: the person is part of your organization. A colleague is covered by a team seat, not by delegation: see [Organization and team](/en/docs/getting-started/organization).
- **Your seat is paid by your organization**: you can then only give access to a colleague of your organization who has a paid plan.
- **Your own address**.
- **Someone already has access**: an invitation is pending or an access is active. Remove it before giving another.
- **Limit reached**: 5 invitations per 24 hours.

Access can also be suspended later: if the person no longer meets the conditions, the screen shows **Access suspended: this person no longer has a paid plan**.

## Remove an access

1. Open **Settings**, then **Access to my account**.
2. Click **Remove access**, then confirm.

It takes effect immediately: the person's session stops, and they can no longer do anything in your account. What you had them set up during the access (a campaign, a mailbox) stays in your account.

The same page lets you cancel an invitation that has not been accepted yet.

## What is recorded

Actions the invited person takes in your account (any change, and contact exports) are recorded in **Settings**, **AI**, **Activity** tab ("AI activity"). Refused attempts appear there too. In the **Tool** column, these lines start with `DELEGATE`.

## Be careful

> **Only invite someone you trust.** They see all the emails of the connected mailboxes, including messages from other services received at those addresses, such as sign-in or password reset links. Remove the access as soon as it is no longer needed.

**Resetting your FluenzR password ends the accesses you gave**, whether active or pending. You will need to invite the person again.
