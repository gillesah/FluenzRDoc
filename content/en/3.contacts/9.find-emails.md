# Email finder

FluenzR finds the work email of a contact who has none, from their first or last name, their company and their LinkedIn profile if known. A contact needs **at least a first name or a last name** to be searched.

## 50 searches included per month

Your plan includes **50 email searches per month**.

- A search is counted only if an address is **found and verified** (the domain receives mail, the address is not disposable). A search with no result, or an unverified address, is neither saved nor counted.
- The counter resets on the 1st of the month. It is shown in **Settings**, **Integrations**, **Enrichment** tab ("N / 50 used this month").
- When the 50 are used, searches stop until the 1st of the next month. This cap also applies if you have connected your own keys.

An address that already belongs to another of your contacts is not saved.

## Searching for one contact

On the page of a contact with no email, use the search button next to the email field (tooltip "Find email via Apollo/Hunter": it also uses the included service). It appears when an Apollo or Hunter key is connected or included searches are available. The address found is written on the page, with its source and the date it was found.

## Bulk search

1. In **Contacts**, tick the contacts you want.
2. Click **Find email (N)**. Up to 1,000 contacts at a time.
3. Contacts that already have an email, or have neither a first nor a last name, are skipped: FluenzR says how many searches are started and how many are skipped.

The search runs gradually, a few contacts at a time. Progress is shown ("12/40 processed, 5 found") then the result. If the monthly quota is reached on the way, the remaining searches are cancelled and the result says how many were not done.

## On a LinkedIn source

When a campaign is fed by a LinkedIn search (see [LinkedIn search](/docs/linkedin/search)), tick **Find the work email of contacts without one**. Every imported contact without an email is searched automatically, with the same rules: work emails only, counted only if the address is found and verified.

## Your Apollo and Hunter keys

In **Settings**, **Integrations**, **Enrichment** tab, you can add your own **Apollo.io** and/or **Hunter.io** API key and choose the preferred provider. Providers are tried in this order: the preferred one, the other, then the included search service. If your key is invalid, the next one is tried. A search made with your keys uses your credits at Apollo or Hunter.
