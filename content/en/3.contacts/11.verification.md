# Address verification

Sending to a mailbox that does not exist causes a bounce, and bounces damage the reputation of your sending mailbox. FluenzR therefore checks your addresses at two moments: **at import** and **just before the first email** of a campaign.

## Address statuses

Every contact has a status, shown as a badge in the contact list and on the contact record (with the date, the source, the service and the detail of the result).

| Status | Meaning | Sending |
|---|---|---|
| **Not verified** | No service has answered for this address yet. | Yes, flagged "not verified" |
| **Valid** | The mailbox exists. | Yes |
| **Invalid** | The service says the mailbox does not exist. | **Never** |
| **No mail server** / **Disposable** | The domain does not receive mail, or it is a temporary address (free checks). | **Never** |
| **Risky** | Full mailbox, role address, poor quality. | According to your setting (off by default) |
| **Catch-all** | The domain accepts every message, so the mailbox cannot be confirmed. | According to your setting (on by default) |
| **Unverifiable** | The service could not reach a conclusion. | According to your setting (on by default) |

The **source** tells where the status comes from: free check (disposable domain, mail server), your key, or "marked valid" by you. A "valid" from the free checks alone only proves the domain receives mail, not that the mailbox exists, so it shows no green badge.

## When an address is verified

- **At import** (CSV, Apollo, HubSpot, Pipedrive, API, AI connector, LinkedIn when an address is found): the free checks are immediate. Verification by a service then runs **in the background**, a few addresses at a time, so a 5,000-row import never blocks the screen.
- **Before the first email** of a campaign: at launch, when contacts are added to a running campaign, and when a dynamic group enrolls a contact. A contact's first email waits at most 6 hours for its verification to finish; after that it goes out anyway.

A contact who already received an email of the sequence is never postponed. Campaigns launched before this feature arrived are not verified after the fact.

A valid, risky or catch-all result is kept for **90 days** for the same address: it is not checked again before then. An "unverifiable" address is retried once after 7 days. An invalid address stays invalid as long as the address does not change. When you change a contact's address, its status goes back to "not verified".

## Your MillionVerifier, ZeroBounce or Bouncer key

In **Settings**, **Integrations**, **Enrichment** tab, **Address verification** block:

1. Choose your service and paste your API key.
2. FluenzR checks it with the service (balance call). A refused key is not saved.
3. The key is encrypted, then masked: it is never displayed again or written to a log.

Verifications made with your key use **your credits** at the service. Only the email address is sent to it: no name, no company, no FluenzR identifier. A result is never shared from one account to another: only colleagues in the same organization benefit from an address that has already been verified.

The verifications **included** in your plan are coming soon: the counter shows "Included verifications: coming soon".

## At import: when the batch is bigger than what is available

With **your key**, the whole batch is queued for verification. Without a key, nothing is used without your knowledge: the import screen shows "4,700 addresses to verify, 0 available" and you choose:

- **Use my key** (taking you to Integrations);
- **Later**: the addresses will be verified before the first send if a verification is available.

When the included verifications are offered, a batch bigger than what remains will also show **Verify the first ones**, which verifies as many as what is available allows.

Through the API and the AI connector, the response carries a `verification` block (`queued`, `notQueued`, `remaining`); the progress of a batch is read with `GET /v1/email-verification/batch/{id}`.

## What FluenzR does with an invalid address

- It is **excluded**: refused when adding to a campaign, skipped by automatic enrollment, refused for a direct send.
- If an address is found invalid just before a send, **the contact's sequence stops**, as after a bounce (all of its running sequences), with a readable reason on its line in the campaign, for example "Adresse invalide (vérifiée le 21/11 par Bouncer)" (the reason is written in French in the current version). The contact is not switched to "bounced".
- A false negative? On the contact record, **Mark as valid** puts the address back in service (source "marked valid by you", with the date and your name). **Verify now** starts the verification again.

## Risky, catch-all, unverifiable: your settings

In **Settings**, **Prospecting rules**:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Verify addresses at import | On |
| Send to risky addresses | Off |
| Send to catch-all addresses | On |
| Send to unverifiable addresses | On |

A risky, catch-all or unverifiable address whose setting is off is **refused as soon as it is added** to a campaign (manual add, API, AI connector). For contacts enrolled by a group, or whose address is only verified after being added, the settings apply to the contact's **first email** in the campaign. A contact that is not sent because of a setting leaves the campaign with a message naming the setting, in your account's language.

## When nothing is available

Without a key or a quota, or if the verification service is down, **sending is never blocked**: you cannot always remove the cause. The contacts concerned carry the "not verified" badge, the campaign header says "N contacts will be sent without address verification", you get a notification in the bell (once a month, never an email), and the AI connector is warned. Add your key to have them verified. The canary batch (the first 20 emails, then a 48-hour bounce measurement) remains your protection against a poor-quality list.
