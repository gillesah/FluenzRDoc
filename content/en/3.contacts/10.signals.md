# Intent signals

A signal is a change at one of your contacts that may justify reaching out: they changed jobs, they reacted to one of your LinkedIn posts, their company raised capital. FluenzR spots them and shows them. There is no cost per signal.

## Signals detected

| Type | Source |
|------|--------|
| Job change | LinkedIn |
| Company change | LinkedIn |
| Reaction to one of your posts | LinkedIn |
| Comment on one of your posts | LinkedIn |
| Establishment opened | BODACC (public data) |
| Capital change or increase | BODACC (public data) |
| Other change to the company | BODACC (public data) |
| Head office moved | Company directory (public data) |
| Leadership change | Company directory (public data) |

LinkedIn signals need a connected LinkedIn account. Only contacts in your CRM are tracked (active or in a pipeline), never unsubscribed ones. For reactions and comments, only those from your contacts are kept: the others are ignored and never stored.

## Where to see them

- On the **Home**, in the **Signals** feed.
- On the **contact page**, which lists their signals.

Each signal reads as a sentence ("changed role", with the previous value) and offers **See the source**.

## Acting on a signal

- **Add to a campaign**: choose the campaign. A contact already active in another campaign, or who cannot be added (unsubscribed, already replied, rejected address...), is not added and FluenzR tells you why.
- **Create a task** on the contact's page.
- **Ignore**.

## Starting a campaign automatically

You can create a rule: "when this type of signal arrives, add the contact to this campaign". It applies to **one contact** (from their page) or to **all tracked contacts** (in **Settings**, **Signals**). Only the contact is added: the campaign's sequence and settings never change.

## Settings

In **Settings**, **Signals**:

- **Turn signals on**: when off, nothing is read anymore (LinkedIn or public data) and the feed disappears from the Home;
- choose the signal types to follow.

## Reading pace

So that a LinkedIn account is never solicited more than an attentive human would:

- a LinkedIn profile is re-read **at most once a week**, spread out, during your sending hours, and never beyond 80 reads per day per account (this budget is shared with LinkedIn imports and searches);
- your posts are collected at most every 12 hours, over your 5 latest posts less than 21 days old;
- company data is re-read at most once a week.
