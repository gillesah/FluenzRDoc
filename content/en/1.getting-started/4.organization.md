# Organization and team

An **organization** brings several sales reps together under one subscription. Each rep has their own (unlimited) mailboxes and their own LinkedIn account, included. The price is 29 EUR excl. VAT per month for the first rep, then 24 EUR excl. VAT per additional rep (see [Plans and pricing](/docs/getting-started/plans)).

> **Note**
> The Organization page is currently displayed in French only. Its labels are given here in English, with the French label in parentheses when it helps.

## Creating the organization

Open **Settings**, then **Organization & Brand**, **My organization** tab (Mon organisation), give it a name and click **Create** (Créer). Creating one is reserved for subscribers: the trial does not allow creating a team.

## Roles

| Role | What they do | Paid seat |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| **Sales rep** (Commercial) | Prospects with their mailboxes and LinkedIn | Yes |
| **Manager (admin)** | Manages the team, seats, invitations and contact assignment | Yes |
| **Read-only** | Looks, without prospecting or LinkedIn | No, free |

## Seats

The **Seats** banner (Places de commercial) shows seats **used**, **free** and **purchased** (Utilisées, Libres, Achetées), with the price for the period (29 then 24 EUR excl. VAT).

- To add or remove a seat, an admin uses the **+** and **-** buttons: FluenzR shows the change to the recurring invoice and the estimated proration Stripe will bill, before you confirm with **Buy** or **Remove** (Acheter, Retirer).
- A seat occupied by a rep, or reserved by a pending invitation, cannot be removed: first remove the rep or cancel the invitation.
- You can have up to 100 seats.

## Inviting a sales rep

1. In **Invite a sales rep** (Inviter un commercial), enter the email and choose the role.
2. Click **Invite** (Inviter). If there is no free seat, the button becomes **Buy a seat and invite** (Acheter une place et inviter): the purchase is included in the invitation. A read-only user does not take a seat.
3. The person receives an email with a link valid for 7 days; they accept and join the organization. A pending invitation reserves a seat.

In **Pending invitations** (admins), each invitation offers:

- **Resend**: the email goes out again with the same link, for 7 more days;
- **Change email**: the invitation is cancelled and a new one is sent to the corrected address, with the same role and the same seat;
- **Cancel**: the link stops working. You can tick **Also release the seat** to take the seat off the invoice; with no Stripe subscription attached, only the seat counter changes.

An expired invitation is shown as such and can be resent.

## Personal or team contacts

In an organization, each contact is **Personal** or **Team**.

- **Personal**: visible only to its sales rep and to admins.
- **Team**: visible to the whole team.

By default, a sales rep creates personal contacts; an admin creates team contacts. Contacts that existed before teams were introduced stayed visible to the whole organization. Outside an organization (single account), none of this applies.

### Sharing or making personal

- **A batch of contacts**: tick them in **Contacts**, then **Share with the team** or **Make personal**.
- **A whole list**: the list's share button applies the visibility to all its contacts.
- **One contact**: the same button on its page.

Only the contact's creator, its assigned sales rep and admins can change visibility.

### Assigned sales rep

Each contact has an **assigned salesperson** (by default the one who created it). An admin can change it with **Assign to...**, for one contact or a batch. The chosen person must be a member of the organization and not read-only. The **All salespeople** filter in the contact list shows only one rep's contacts.

### "Already followed by..."

To prevent two reps from prospecting the same person, FluenzR compares the email with those of the organization's contacts. If a colleague already follows this contact, it is not duplicated and a warning appears: "Already followed by First Last". On import, the contacts concerned are skipped and the result says "N contact(s) skipped because a colleague already follows them".
