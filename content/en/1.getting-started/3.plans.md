# Plans and pricing

FluenzR has a single plan, **Pro**, and there is no free plan any more.

## The Pro plan, per sales rep

The price is calculated **per sales rep** (one named user):

| | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---------|--------|
| First sales rep | **29 EUR excl. VAT** (34.80 EUR incl. VAT) | **290 EUR excl. VAT** |
| Each additional sales rep | **24 EUR excl. VAT** (28.80 EUR incl. VAT) | **240 EUR excl. VAT** |

Annual equals 10 months for 12; no team discount is added on top.

Each sales rep gets:

- unlimited sending mailboxes, contacts and sends, within what your provider allows (Gmail, Outlook, SMTP): no cap set by FluenzR;
- mailbox warming, A/B tests, CRM and Inbox;
- **their own LinkedIn account included**: profile visit, invitation, message, InMail and engagement (see [LinkedIn](/docs/linkedin));
- the Calendar and its booking page (see [Calendar](/docs/calendar));
- 50 email searches per month (see [Email finder](/docs/contacts/find-emails));
- connecting an AI (Claude, ChatGPT) through the MCP server (see [MCP](/docs/api/mcp)).

One subscription corresponds to one named user: credentials are not shared.

## Team and read-only users

An additional sales rep is paid for from the **Organization** page (see [Organization and team](/docs/getting-started/organization)). **Read-only users** (for example a manager) are free and have no LinkedIn account.

## Need another LinkedIn account?

The "extra LinkedIn account at 10 EUR excl. VAT per month" add-on is no longer sold. For another LinkedIn account, invite a sales rep: 24 EUR excl. VAT per month from the 2nd, with their unlimited mailboxes and their LinkedIn included. Subscribers who already had extra LinkedIn accounts at 10 EUR excl. VAT keep them as they are.

## 14-day trial

- You try the Pro plan for 14 days.
- No card is asked for email. A card is requested **only to connect a LinkedIn account** during the trial; it is not charged before the trial ends.
- If you do not subscribe when the trial ends, the account is paused: your data is kept, but nothing is sent.

## LANCEMENT19 code

The code **LANCEMENT19** takes 10 EUR excl. VAT off per month, for life, on the monthly subscription (19 EUR excl. VAT per month). It is limited to the first 20 users and does not apply to the annual plan.

## Subscribing and managing your subscription

1. Open your **Profile** (bottom-left icon).
2. Click **Manage my subscription**.
3. Pay or change your subscription through Stripe: invoices, payment method and cancellation are available there.

You can cancel at any time, with no commitment.

## Earlier subscribers

Subscribers on an older price keep it, with 1 LinkedIn account included. Existing free accounts keep their access.
