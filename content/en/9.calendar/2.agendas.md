# Connect a calendar

In **Calendar**, **Settings**, **Availability** tab, the **Connected calendars** section lists your calendars. A slot that is busy in any of them disappears from your booking page.

## Google Calendar

Click **Connect** next to Google Calendar and authorise FluenzR. You get:

- reading of your busy times, to avoid conflicts;
- automatic adding of meetings to that calendar (the **Add meetings to this calendar** option);
- a **Google Meet** link created at booking, for Google Meet meeting types.

## Outlook

Same principle with Outlook (personal and work accounts): conflicts, automatic adding of meetings and a **Microsoft Teams** meeting for Teams types. Outlook itself sends the invitation to the prospect. The Teams meeting is only created if the Microsoft account is a work account.

## Another calendar (iCal address)

Paste your calendar's secret iCal address and click **Add**. FluenzR reads your busy times without being able to write to it: the .ics invitation then comes from your sending mailbox.

- Google Calendar: Calendar settings, Integrate calendar, Secret address in iCal format.
- Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, ICS link.
- iCloud: Calendar, share, Public calendar, webcal link.

## Good to know

- Busy times are re-read about every 10 minutes, and at each booking. The **Refresh** button forces an update.
- Only one calendar receives meetings: the one where **Add meetings to this calendar** is ticked. It is ticked when a Google or Outlook calendar is connected if no other calendar already receives meetings, and ticking it on another calendar unticks it elsewhere.
- If access to a calendar expires, a banner says so in the Calendar and the source switches to **Reconnect**: click **Reconnect**. Until it is reconnected, its busy times are no longer read.
- **Remove** deletes the calendar from FluenzR: its busy times are no longer taken into account.
