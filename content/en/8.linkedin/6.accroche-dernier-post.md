# Hook from the latest post

The **accroche_post** variable inserts a short sentence about the prospect's latest post into a message. Use it in the **LinkedIn message after acceptance** and in the **email**. Avoid the invitation note (300 characters, and it reads better without a note).

## How it works

1. The day before sending, FluenzR reads the prospect's latest **original post**, published less than 30 days ago. A plain reshare, a post without text or an old post does not count.
2. FluenzR's AI writes a sentence of 25 words at most, in the campaign language. It invents nothing: it talks about the subject of the post, without flattery, links, or "I noticed". A sensitive post (bereavement, illness, layoffs, job offer, politics) is set aside.
3. Each hook costs **1 AI credit**.
4. Depending on your setting, you review it before sending, or it goes out directly.

## Insert the hook in a message

In the editor of an email step or a LinkedIn message, click **Insert the latest-post hook**. FluenzR adds the sentence **with a fallback text** that you can edit:

`{if accroche_post}{{accroche_post}}{/if}{if !accroche_post}I'm writing to you about your business.{/if}`

The fallback text is mandatory: FluenzR refuses to save a step that uses the variable without it. It is sent when:

- the prospect has no usable post (none, too old, plain reshare, sensitive subject);
- the prospect has no LinkedIn address, or you have no LinkedIn account connected;
- the LinkedIn account's read budget is used up for the day (80 per day);
- your monthly AI credits are used up;
- the hook was rejected or not reviewed in time.

Sending is never blocked.

## Review hooks before sending

On the campaign page, the **Review hooks before sending** box is **checked by default**.

- **Checked**: tomorrow's hooks arrive on the campaign page the day before (a notification appears in the bell). For each one you see the prospect, an excerpt of the post and its link. Click **Approve**, edit the text then **Approve my text**, or **Reject**: a rejected hook is replaced by the fallback text. **Approve all** approves the rest in one click. A hook not reviewed by sending time goes out with the fallback text.
- **Unchecked**: hooks go out without review.

## Good to know

- Posts are read through your LinkedIn account, within the same budget as the **Like a post** step: a prospect is read only once a day for both. A very large email campaign cannot have one hook per send: the surplus goes out with the fallback text.
- The text of the post is not stored: only the hook, the post address and its date are.
