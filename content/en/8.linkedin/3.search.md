# LinkedIn search

## One-off search

1. Open **LinkedIn**, **Search** tab.
2. Choose the LinkedIn account (and Sales Navigator if the account has it).
3. Fill in the criteria: keywords, job title, location, company, industry, degree of connection (1st, 2nd, 3rd and beyond).
4. Tick the people you want, then **Add to a list**.
5. Add the list to a campaign (new or existing).

Only public profile information is kept, never email or phone. To protect your account, the number of results shown and imported per day is limited; the counter shows what is left.

## Import an existing search

The **Import from LinkedIn** button (Contacts) accepts the link of a people search, a Sales Navigator search or a lead list. 200 profiles by default, 1,000 at most; a large import continues automatically the next day.

## Automation: a search that feeds a campaign

In the campaign editor, choose **A LinkedIn search** as the source. New profiles enter every day according to your criteria (1 to 15 invitations a day). The default sequence: profile visit, invitation with a note, message one day after acceptance, follow-up three days later; without acceptance, the invitation is withdrawn.

**Find the work email of contacts without one** (checkbox on the source) starts an email search for each imported profile, using your included searches (see [Email finder](/docs/contacts/find-emails)).

### Several searches on the same account

An account's number of invitations per day is limited. When several active searches feed campaigns with the **same account**, that cap is **shared between them in proportion to their targets**. If the sum of the targets fits within the cap, each keeps its target; otherwise each search only fills its share of the invitation queue. On a new account with the [gradual ramp-up](/docs/linkedin) ticked, the source screen shows the target and the number of invitations for today during the ramp-up.

The **Automations** tab lists these searches with their state (active, paused, waiting for the account, search exhausted...) and counters (imported, invited, accepted). Pausing stops new contacts from being added; those already in the campaign carry on.
