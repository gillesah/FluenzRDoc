# LinkedIn steps in a sequence

A campaign can mix email and LinkedIn in the same workflow editor. Click **LinkedIn step** to add an action.

## Actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Visit profile** | Opens the contact's profile. They are notified of your visit. |
| **Invite to connect** | Sends a connection request, with or without a note. |
| **Send a message** | Writes to someone already connected to you. If the invitation is not accepted, the step is skipped. |
| **Send an InMail** | Writes to someone outside your network and uses an InMail credit. Without credit, the step is skipped. |
| **Withdraw the invitation** | Withdraws the pending invitation if it was not accepted. Place it after the "invitation accepted" wait. |
| **Like a recent post** | Reacts with a Like to the person's most recent post, if it is under 30 days old (never a repost, never a comment). With no recent post, the step is skipped and the sequence carries on. |
| **Follow the profile** | Follows the person without inviting. Place it before the invitation. |
| **Voice note** | Sends your voice note (2 minutes at most) to someone who accepted your invitation. |

## "Like" step: good to know

The **Like a recent post** step only reads the person's latest posts: it picks the most recent original post published less than 30 days ago. It is limited to 20 Likes per day per account. The **Warm-up** sequence template uses it: visit, one post liked and a follow before inviting, to get known gently.

## Writing a step

For the invitation, message and InMail you write a text with first name, last name and company variables. With a free LinkedIn account, the invitation note is limited to 200 characters and about 5 notes a month. Contacts without a LinkedIn URL skip the step.

Invitations go out from 8:30 am to 6:00 pm, spread over the day and limited per account (see [Connect a LinkedIn account](/docs/linkedin)).

## "Invitation accepted" condition

Add the condition **LinkedIn invitation accepted**: yes as soon as the invitation is accepted, no if it is not by the deadline. You can then send a LinkedIn message on the yes branch, and an email or the withdrawal of the invitation on the no branch.

## Accounts used

When the campaign starts, tick the LinkedIn accounts to use. None ticked means all your active accounts are used. A contact keeps the same account for the whole sequence.

## Tracking

The campaign shows **LinkedIn tracking**: visits, invitations, accepted, messages, InMails, replies, and a per-step detail. On a contact's page, the **LinkedIn history** lists each action with its result. LinkedIn replies arrive in the Inbox, which can be filtered by channel.
