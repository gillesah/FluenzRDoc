# Posts to comment on

On the home page, the **Posts to comment on** card lists the recent posts of the prospects in your LinkedIn campaigns, each with a comment suggested by the AI.

## How it works

1. FluenzR reads the latest post of your active prospects. When a campaign has a **Like a post** step, the same read serves both the like and the comment.
2. The AI suggests a comment that adds a point of view or a question, in the language of the post, with no link and no sales pitch. If the post does not lend itself to a comment (a plain share, a post that is too old, a sensitive topic), there is no suggestion.
3. Edit the text if you like, then click **Copy and open the post**: the comment is copied and the post opens in a new tab. Paste it and publish it yourself.
4. Click **It's published** once done, or **Skip** to dismiss it.

## With the FluenzR Chrome extension

If the FluenzR extension (version 1.2 or later) is installed, an **Open and comment** button appears: the post opens with the comment box already filled in. The extension never clicks Publish, you do; it then detects the publication and marks the suggestion as done. On a post or profile page it also shows the pending suggestion for that prospect. Without the extension, use **Copy and open the post**.

## Good to know

- **FluenzR never posts a comment for you**: LinkedIn forbids comments posted by a third-party tool.
- 10 suggestions per day by default, adjustable from 1 to 20 on the card. Each suggestion uses 1 AI credit.
- Always reread: a comment that could sit under any post gets less reach on LinkedIn.
- The text of the post is kept only while the suggestion is pending. It is erased when you click **It's published** or **Skip**, and after 14 days.
